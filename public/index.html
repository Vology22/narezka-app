Narezka API

A publishing tool for short-form video creators.

Narezka API helps creators prepare and publish short vertical videos to their own TikTok account. Videos are made from source material that is in the public domain, released under an open license (such as Creative Commons), or owned by the creator. The source and license of every video are recorded in the app.

Open the app

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The creator selects a prepared video and reviews its preview, title and caption.
  3. The creator chooses privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings and confirms the post.
  4. The app publishes the video to the creator's account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Nothing is posted without the creator's explicit confirmation, and the creator can disconnect the app at any time.
