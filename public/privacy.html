Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok we receive, with your permission, your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) and an access token that lets the app publish to your account (scope video.publish). We also keep the videos, captions and settings you prepare in the app.

2. How we use it

We use this data only to identify your account inside the app and to publish the videos you confirm. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the actions you request. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the Service.

4. Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. You can also ask us to delete the data we hold about you by writing to the address below; we will do so within 30 days.

5. Children

The Service is not intended for people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
